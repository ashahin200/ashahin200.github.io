About

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at North Carolina A&T State University , working in Gaire’s Interdisciplinary Lab under the supervision of Dr. Sunil Kumar Gaire . My doctoral research focuses on developing machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision methods for biomedical imaging applications in Cancer Research & Alzheimer's Diseases . I am also interested in developing practical, reliable image-analysis methods that support scientific discovery and clinical research.

Research direction & ECE interests

My research centers on AI and deep learning for image analysis and biomedical applications. My broader ECE interests include image and signal processing, data analysis, and the development of data-driven computational methods.

AI & deep learning

Machine learning, computer vision, and neural-network methods for classification and segmentation.

Image processing

Biomedical image analysis, microscopy, MRI, and quantitative image interpretation.

Signal processing & data analysis

Interests in extracting useful information from imaging signals and data through computational analysis.
